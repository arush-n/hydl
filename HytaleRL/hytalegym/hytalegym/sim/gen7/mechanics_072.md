# Gen-7 constructs: what 0.7.2 does that the gen-5 engine does not (design, 2026-10-17)

A construct is one behaviour, implemented once in the engine behind one field of
`hytalegym.sim.common.profile.Constructs`. Gen 5 keeps every default (today's behaviour); gen 7
sets the field (`sim/gen7/profile.py`). The stubs in `mechanics/` are typed, unwired interfaces;
`mechanics.require_construct(profile, field)` raises `NotImplementedError` until a construct is
implemented, so a gen-7 render cannot quietly run with gen-5 behaviour. Under the owner's render
rule the gen-5 source is never edited: a render copies it from `git show HEAD:<path>`, applies the
redirect list (`sim/common/content/redirects.json`), and inserts a hook where a construct runs.

Gaps and measurements come from `HytaleRL/hytale-plugin-0.7.2/FIDELITY-0.7.2.md` (rows cited as
F1a, F5a, ...). Inputs name gen-7 content-table rows (`family/row#field`) that exist now (asserted
by `tests/sim/test_constructs.py`).

**Construct (gen 7).** `tick_ability_programs` is wrapped (`overlay.py` hook `ability_ground_continuation`). Gen 5 returns the original (switch off, the default).

**Construct (`mechanics/npc_jump_carry.py`).** On an accepted npc_walk jump with horizontal speed > 0 the force velocity is set to
the take-off horizontal velocity and the counter `CombatState.agent_jump_carry_ticks` (patches `jump_carry_state_field`,
`jump_carry_state_init`) to 1. The counter rises by one on each tick that starts grounded. On L+4 (counter 5) the force's horizontal
part is multiplied by 0.45; on L+5 (counter 6) the force is cleared, so the walk resumes with the held move speed 5.0 at once. The
A knockback during the carry (the knockback control lock is set) clears the counter, so the knockback's own force is neither
scaled on L+4 nor cleared on L+5. The hook is `_apply_action` (`combat/runtime/step.py`), listed before the landing hook, so the
landing walk stays innermost.

**Decision: `landing_walk_resume` is kept for standing jumps (zero take-off speed) and is not retired.** The only measured L+3
evidence is a standing jump (saved capture `single-jump-30`; `tests/sim/test_landing_walk.py` pins it) and the old running jump,
whose horizontal momentum was zero at take-off. A standing jump is not in the new receipt, so its L+3 is inferred to be unchanged
on the new bridge. With the switch on, the landing hold arms only when the take-off speed is zero; a running jump is carried. The
walk-off landing (no jump, walks on L+1) and the knockback landing (walks on L+2, the engine's control lock) are unchanged.

**Not explained and not measured.** (1) The L+4 factor 0.45 is fitted to one live value (0.203; the ground factor alone gives
0.452, so the live L+4 is 0.25 blocks/s lower than the damping predicts). No HEAD term gives 0.45 here (the deadzone 0.1 and the
movement resistance 0.242 do not), and the coincidence with `knockback_scale` 0.5 times 0.9 is unverified. (2) A standing jump on the
new bridge (assumed to keep L+3). (3) A jump from a walk ramp (speed 0 < v < 5): the carry follows the engine's damping, not measured.
(4) A re-jump during a carry: the take-off takes the current velocity; not measured. (5) Steering in the air: the receipt shows none
for the one direction measured (-z). (6) A knockback during a carry: the counter clears (the guard above); the knockback's own
decay is the engine's, and no live receipt covers a hit in the air during a running jump.
